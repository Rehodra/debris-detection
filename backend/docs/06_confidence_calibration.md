# Component Guide: Multi-Source Confidence Calibration & Explainability

The **Confidence Calibration Service** ([`confidence_service.py`](file:///Users/priyangshu/Desktop/Coding/debris/debris-detection/backend/app/services/confidence_service.py)) fuses AI neural probabilities, acoustic shadow evidence, and local image quality into calibrated operational trust tiers with plain-language explainability notes.

---

## The Three Pillars of Confidence

Standard computer vision relies solely on the raw softmax probability of the neural network. In underwater sonar environments, speckle noise, seabed ripples, and gas seeps frequently fool neural networks. MarineScan implements a tripartite fusion model:

```
                  ┌───────────────────────────────┐
                  │ 1. AI Confidence (45%)        │
                  │    Raw YOLO Softmax Output    │
                  └───────────────┬───────────────┘
                                  │
                  ┌───────────────┴───────────────┐
                  │ 2. Physics Score (35%)        │
                  │    Shadow Evidence (55%)      │
                  │    Dimensional Plausibility   │
                  └───────────────┬───────────────┘
                                  │
                  ┌───────────────┴───────────────┐
                  │ 3. Image Quality (20%)        │
                  │    Local ROI Sharpness,       │
                  │    Contrast & Exposure Gain   │
                  └───────────────┬───────────────┘
                                  │
                                  ▼
                     Calibrated Trust Tier (0 - 1)
                     + Plain-Language Explanations
```

---

## Fusion Algorithm & Dynamic Noise Gating

$$C_{\text{calibrated}} = 0.45 \cdot C_{\text{AI}} + 0.35 \cdot C_{\text{Phys}} + 0.20 \cdot C_{\text{Qual}}$$

### Dynamic Turbidity / Noise Gating
When local acoustic quality is severely degraded ($C_{\text{Qual}} < 0.40$):
- Neural network feature extraction becomes unreliable.
- The fusion engine dynamically shifts reliance toward physical shadow geometry:
  $$w_{\text{AI}} = 0.30, \quad w_{\text{Phys}} = 0.50, \quad w_{\text{Qual}} = 0.20$$

### Dimensional Plausibility Penalty Multiplier
If an object violates physical naval architecture boundaries (e.g. a $200\text{m}$ aircraft or $5\text{cm}$ shipwreck):
- The physical score drops to $0.0$.
- A **$0.55\times$ penalty multiplier** is applied across the fused confidence, forcing the target into ambiguous or false-alarm tiers.

## Operational Trust Tiers

| Trust Tier | Confidence Range | Action Directive |
|---|---|---|
| `VERIFIED_TARGET` | $\ge 0.80$ | High-confidence debris with confirmed acoustic shadow and plausible physical dimensions. Auto-logged for salvage/charting. |
| `PROBABLE_DEBRIS` | $0.60 - 0.79$ | Likely debris; corroborating evidence exists but shadow or image quality is moderately noisy. Scheduled for ROV pass. |
| `AMBIGUOUS_ANOMALY` | $0.40 - 0.59$ | Seabed anomaly requiring human hydrographer review before dispatching surface or subsea assets. |
| `SUSPECTED_FALSE_ALARM` | $< 0.40$ | Lacks acoustic shadow corroboration or violates physical dimension rules. Filtered from high-priority alert queues. |

## Plain-Language Explainability Rationale

Every target generated by the pipeline includes an `explanation_notes` list explaining why confidence was increased or decreased:
- *"High-contrast acoustic shadow confirms significant 3D seabed elevation (+0.18 boost)."*
- *"Acoustic shadow direction aligns radially with sidescan nadir track."*
- *"Dimensions are fully consistent with known naval architectural boundaries for shipwreck."*
- *"Degraded local acoustic quality: increased reliance on physical shadow corroboration."*
- *"Target lacks corroborating acoustic shadow; risk of 2D seabed anomaly or biological noise."*

## API Endpoints

- **`POST /api/v1/confidence/evaluate-target`**: Compute multi-pillar fusion and trust tier for a specific target.
- **`POST /api/v1/confidence/analyze-image`**: Execute end-to-end detection + shadow + physics + confidence calibration.
